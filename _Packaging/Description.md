Extended Vendor UI is an addon that enhances the default vendor window with customizable layouts and powerful filtering options to help you find exactly what you're looking for.

Works on Retail, Mists of Pandaria Classic, Burning Crusade Anniversary, Classic Era and WoW Forever (not with its gamepad interface).

### Features
- **Customizable Layout**: Extend the default 5 rows, 2 columns vendor window up to 99 rows and 99 columns.
- **Fill Direction**: Choose whether items fill the window in rows first or columns first.
- **Search Box**: Quickly find items by name, optionally kept when you reopen the vendor or move on to another one.
- **Filters**: Show only one kind of item: pets, mounts, toys, appearances (by armor and weapon type), appearance sets, illusions, recipes or housing decor. The last filter you used can be remembered.
- **Custom Filter**: Create your own filter by combining multiple item types:
  - Show only specific item types (pets, mounts, toys, etc.)
  - Filter appearances by armor type (cloth, leather, mail, plate, cosmetic, shield)
  - Filter appearances by weapon type (axes, swords, maces, bows, guns, wands, etc.)
- **Hide Collected**: Hide items you've already collected:
  - Pets
  - Mounts
  - Toys
  - Appearances
  - Appearance sets (ensembles and arsenals)
  - Recipes, and optionally recipes your other characters know once they have opened a vendor that sells them
  - Housing decor, in storage or placed (with configurable quantity threshold from 1-999)
- **Gear Filters**: For weapons and armor: only usable items, hide armor below the best type your class can wear, and a minimum quality.
- **Token Banner**: The total cost of the items in the list (it follows the filter and search) and what you have, for gold, currencies and items. Choose need, have or both and how money and currencies are formatted, show only the gold you have, or hide currencies you have none of.
- **Bulk Purchase**: Shift-click an item to buy several stacks at once. Shows how many you can afford, how many fit in your bags and how many the vendor has, with an optional confirmation.
- **Easy Access**: Options button on the vendor window for quick adjustments, or use the full options menu via minimap/addon compartment icon.

The pet, appearance set, illusion and recipe filters are on Retail and WoW Forever, the housing filter on Retail only.

### Fully translated into Chinese (Simplified), mostly into Russian, and partly into German, French and Italian

### Compatibility
- ElvUI and GW2 UI (not yet on WoW Forever)
- EllesmereUI (with its merchant list mode on, Extended Vendor UI stays off)
- Can I Mog It? (CIMI)
- Transmog Loot Helper (12.1.5-02 or later)
- Auctionator, TradeSkillMaster (TSM) and Zygor read every item of the vendor, also while a filter is active
